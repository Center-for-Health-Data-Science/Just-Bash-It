How to make banana break

Ingredients:
- 100 gr butter
- 4 bananas
- 1 egg
- 200 gr brown sugar
- 220 gr flour
- some walnuts
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- baking soda
- 1 pkg vanilla sugar 
- salt

Instructions:
1. Mash the bananas.
2. Mix with butter, sugar, vanilla sugar and egg.
3. Mix flour, baking soda, cinnamon, salt.
4. Add the flour mix stepwise to the banana mix so that it doesn't clump.
5. Put into a 25 cm or 30 cm cake form.
6. Preheat oven to 180 C.
7. Bake at 180 C for 60 minutes.

Alterations:
- cashew
- almond
- fruit
- whole grain flour
